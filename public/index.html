FAQ

Which browser should I use?

Android: Chrome. This matters more than you'd think: Chrome installs Aimless as a real app — its own icon with no browser badge, its own window, a place in the app drawer, and protected storage. Most other Android browsers only pin a shortcut, and some restrict the sensors or storage Aimless needs.

iPhone: Safari. iOS only lets Safari install apps to the home screen, so open Aimless there first.

How do I install Aimless?

Chrome on Android: open aimless.earth, tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

Safari on iPhone: open aimless.earth, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

After that, always open Aimless from its home-screen icon.

Which settings should I check?

Location (and motion, on iPhone) are asked for on first open — both are essential. If you denied one by mistake:

  • Chrome on Android: tap the icon left of the address bar, then Permissions, and allow Location (and Motion sensors if listed).
  • Safari on iPhone: iOS won't ask twice. Remove Aimless from your home screen, re-add it from Safari, and allow access. Also check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • Brave on Android: two gotchas. Brave blocks motion sensors by default, so the compass arrow never moves — allow them under brave://settings/content/sensors. And "Clear browsing data on exit" (Settings → Privacy) wipes your saved walks every time Brave closes — turn it off, or use Chrome.
How can I export and save my walks?

Open a walk from the Archive and tap "Export as HTML" — you get a single self-contained file that opens anywhere, no app needed. "Share walk" sends the same file to another app.

Important: walks live only in your browser's storage, and browsers can clear it under storage pressure or via privacy settings. Always export and back up any walk you want to keep.

Can I remix my exported walk with my favourite AI chatbot?

Yes! The export is a plain, self-contained HTML file — perfect raw material. Upload it to your chatbot of choice and try prompts like:

  • "Extract the photos from this file and give them back to me as PNGs."
  • "Rewrite the five cards as a short poem about this walk."
  • "Turn the walked shape into a poster, with the date and distance as a caption."
  • "Restyle the page: change the colours to [your palette] and the font to [your font]."
  • "Write a one-paragraph story from these five cards."

Treat the file as a souvenir you can keep remixing — the original never changes.

How can I save a photo from a walk as a standalone image?

One easy way: upload the exported walk HTML file to an AI chatbot and ask it to export the photos as PNG files.

What is the Voice all about?

The voice you pick on the Go screen — The Crow, The Threshold, The Lattice — sets the character of the cards you receive when you reach a waypoint. Each voice offers directives in its own style. You can consider these directives and ponder them, or ignore them.

Tip: it can be a good idea to ponder the voice directives, because they can sometimes manifest a kind of magic synchronicity that only becomes apparent after you pause and consider — give it a go!

Why is there no map?

The absence is the design. Aimless is about being where you are, not navigating somewhere — you follow an arrow, not a route, and the shape of your walk is only revealed afterwards.
